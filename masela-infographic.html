“We Need Money” — and a $20.9 B Gas Bet “我们需要钱”——与一场209亿美元的天然气押注

Prabowo ties health care, schools and teachers’ pay to the Abadi Masela LNG project — whose revenue is still years away. 普拉博沃将医疗、学校与教师工资寄望于阿巴迪马塞拉LNG项目——但其收入仍需数年才能到来。

Kompas.com 16 Jul 2026 · Tanimbar Islands, Maluku 2026年7月16日 · 马鲁古·塔宁巴尔群岛

01 The Story新闻事实

President Prabowo Subianto said Indonesia needs funding for health care, schools, teachers’ salaries, civil-servant pay and infrastructure. Speaking at the inauguration of the Abadi Masela LNG project in the Tanimbar Islands on 16 July 2026, he argued the gas project’s investment can help finance development and underscored the need for energy downstreaming. 普拉博沃·苏比延多总统表示，印尼需要资金用于医疗服务、学校、教师工资、公务员薪酬和基础设施。2026年7月16日，他在塔宁巴尔群岛出席阿巴迪马塞拉LNG项目开工仪式时表示，该天然气项目的投资有助于为国家发展提供资金，并强调了能源下游化的必要性。

  • Prabowo said the state’s funding needs span health care, schools, teachers’ salaries, civil-servant pay and modern infrastructure. 普拉博沃称，国家资金需求涵盖医疗服务、学校、教师工资、公务员薪酬和现代化基础设施。
  • The remarks came at the groundbreaking of the Abadi Masela LNG project in Tanimbar, Maluku, on 16 July 2026. 这一表态发表于2026年7月16日马鲁古塔宁巴尔阿巴迪马塞拉LNG项目的开工仪式上。
  • According to Prabowo, the project’s investment reaches US$20.9 billion, and its gas, condensate and related products will help Indonesia’s development. 据普拉博沃介绍，项目投资达209亿美元，其天然气、凝析油及相关产品将助力印尼的发展。
  • Prabowo framed downstreaming as a necessity that depends on energy availability. 普拉博沃将下游化定位为一项依赖能源供应保障的必需战略。
¶

The government casts Masela as a US$20.9 billion energy project meant to strengthen supply and development revenue — yet its fiscal and commercial payoff remains a medium-term projection, with production targeted for 2029–2030. 政府将马塞拉定位为一项价值209亿美元的能源项目，期望其巩固能源供应并充实发展收入——然而其财政与商业回报仍属中期预测，投产目标定于2029至2030年。

Project Scale vs the Development-Funding Claim 项目规模 vs 发展资金叙事

Masela’s investment equals roughly Rp339 trillion at the government’s assumed rate of Rp16,900 per US dollar. That figure measures the project’s capital scale — not state revenue already available to spend on health or education. 按政府假设汇率（1美元兑16,900印尼盾）计算，马塞拉的投资额约合339万亿印尼盾。这一数字体现的是项目的资本规模，而非已可用于医疗或教育支出的确定国家收入。

5 Contradiction矛盾之处

The Fiscal Payoff Is Not Immediate 财政效益并非立竿见影

The statement about public-funding needs came as the project began — yet the government itself targets construction for 2027 and production for 2029–2030. The project cannot be treated as an immediate cash source for public services in 2026. 关于公共资金需求的表态发表于项目启动之时，但政府自身将建设期定于2027年、投产期定于2029至2030年。因此在2026年，该项目还不能被视为公共服务支出的直接现金来源。

7 Cause因果关系

Gas and the Energy Transition 天然气与能源转型

Carbon capture and storage is built into Masela’s development plan as part of the project design. It can reduce CO₂ separated from the reservoir — but it does not by itself prove the entire LNG value chain is emissions-free. 碳捕集与封存（CCS）作为项目设计的一部分被纳入马塞拉的开发方案。它能减少从气藏中分离出的二氧化碳排放，但并不能由此证明整条LNG价值链是零排放的。

  • The revised Plan of Development approved on 5 December 2023 incorporates carbon capture and storage into the project.2023年12月5日获批的修订版开发计划将碳捕集与封存纳入项目。
  • INPEX says the project’s CCS-related costs qualify for cost recovery under Indonesia’s production sharing contract scheme.INPEX表示，该项目的CCS相关成本符合印尼产品分成合同（PSC）机制下的成本回收条件。
  • ESDM says CCS is applied to support the project’s environmental standards and Indonesia’s net-zero emissions target.能矿部称，应用CCS旨在支撑项目的环保标准以及印尼的净零排放目标。
